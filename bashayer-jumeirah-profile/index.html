02 — Hajj Season 2026

A Lesson from Hajj

With Rawaf Mina Company, I helped international pilgrims with their inquiries, check-in and check-out, and Nusuk activation. One moment from that season has stayed with me.

The need

On a busy Friday, an elderly European pilgrim travelling alone needed to rejoin her group at the Haram for her Umrah arrangements.

Why it mattered

The area was crowded and taxis were hard to reach. She did not know how to use ride-hailing apps, and she had no Saudi Riyals in cash.

What I did
  • Walked with her to the main road.
  • Helped her exchange her euros into Saudi Riyals.
  • Arranged a taxi and confirmed the fare with the driver.
  • Made sure he understood exactly where she needed to go.
The result

She was happy and grateful.

What I learned

Each obstacle was small on its own. Together, they stood between her and her group. Good service meant seeing all of them at once, and staying with her until each one was solved.

Hospitality is not only about completing a service. It is about noticing what a person needs, taking appropriate action, and helping them feel welcomed, heard, and supported.

04 — Jumeirah Jabal Omar Makkah

How I Could Contribute

Jumeirah Jabal Omar Makkah welcomes guests a few minutes’ walk from Masjid Al Haram, many of them on one of the most meaningful journeys of their lives. While researching Jumeirah, I read about its HEART service philosophy. It describes the kind of service I want to give, and I recognise parts of my own experience in it.

H
Human
Helping pilgrims far from home feel welcomed and listened to, rather than simply processed.
E
Engaging
Communicating with people of many backgrounds and personalities — international pilgrims during Hajj, and hundreds of content creators in marketing.
A
Attentive
Campaign coordination trained me to track many small details — deliveries, payments, deadlines — and to notice when one is missing.
R
Responsive
When an elderly guest who did not speak Arabic became unwell, I contacted emergency medical services straight away, accompanied the responders to her room, and stayed while they attended to her.
T
Thoughtful
Afterwards, I brought her ice packs, Panadol and food from the buffet. She thanked me for my help — a reminder of how much a small gesture can mean.
What I would aim to bring
i.

Thoughtful Guest Communication

Listening carefully, understanding what a guest needs, and responding clearly and respectfully — with patience for guests navigating an unfamiliar place.

ii.

Reliable Coordination

Following each request through to closure, keeping track of details, supporting colleagues, and learning the hotel’s procedures and systems properly.

iii.

A Customer-Centred Perspective

Remembering that a stay is shaped by many small interactions, not only the main transaction — and giving each one proper care.

HEART (Human, Engaging, Attentive, Responsive, Thoughtful) is Jumeirah’s published service philosophy. It appears here as my own reading of it; I have not been trained in it, and this profile is not affiliated with Jumeirah.
